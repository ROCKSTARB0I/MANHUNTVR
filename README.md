# ▶ [PLAY DEAD AIR](https://rockstarb0i.github.io/MANHUNTVR/)

**https://rockstarb0i.github.io/MANHUNTVR/** — open this link in the Meta Quest browser and press **Enter VR**.

Carcer City. You are **Josh Earnings**, framed and sentenced to die, then bought out of death row by a man who calls himself the Director. Now you're the star of his snuff film, and every hunter in the district has been paid to kill you.

- **Stealth first.** Your wrist shows how *seen* you are (the light you stand in) and how much *noise* you make. Hunters see you far faster in light and hear running, thrown junk and, if you turn on the microphone, your real voice.
- **Motion-controlled takedowns.** Pull a plastic bag over a head from behind and hold it. Really stab with the glass shard. Really swing the crowbar.
- **Distractions.** Throw bottles and bricks to send hunters to go look.
- **The Director** talks in your ear and watches through CCTV cameras that report you. Smash them.

Built from scratch for this game: its own skinned bodies, verlet ragdolls, light-grid stealth, pathfinding hunters, synthesized sound. Only three.js is borrowed.

Voice lines: see [VOICE_LINES.md](VOICE_LINES.md). Recordings go in `vo/` as `<ID>.mp3` and play automatically.
