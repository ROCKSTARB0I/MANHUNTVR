Stay in the shadows. Stand perfectly still in the dark and nobody can see you, even right next to you. Your wrist (or the meters on screen) says HIDDEN when you are.

Listen. Running is loud and shows as rings on your radar. Tap a wall, or throw a bottle or brick, to lure a Hood out of his route. Radar dots: blue = unaware, yellow = suspicious, red = hunting you.

Executions. Get behind an unaware Hood with a weapon and a reticle appears. Hold to charge it from GREY to GREEN to RED, then let go and act it out: more strikes, or a longer hold with the bag, the higher you charged. Fighting in the open gets you killed.

Quest. Left stick walks (click to sprint), right stick turns, grip picks up and throws, A switches between the weapons you carry, trigger charges executions, B crouches (or really crouch). Knock on walls with your hand. Any button skips the opening tape.

Desktop. WASD, mouse, Shift run, C crouch, E pick up, hold click to charge / click to strike, Q throw, T tap a wall, G drop, 1–4 / mouse wheel switch weapons (X: bare hands), F swap hands, V third-person camera, N free camera.
