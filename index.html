Building Carcer City…

Stay in the dark. Your SEEN meter (on your left wrist in VR) shows how lit you are. Hunters spot you much faster in light. Crouch for less noise and a smaller shape.

Stay quiet. Running is loud. Walking is quiet. Crouched is nearly silent. Throw bottles and bricks to send hunters to go look somewhere else.

Quest. Left stick walks (click it to sprint), right stick turns. Grip picks up whatever your hand is touching; let go while swinging to throw. Bag: reach over a hunter's head from behind with the bag, then hold grip and pull for about four seconds. Glass shard and crowbar: really stab and swing — they only hurt when they're moving fast. Crouch for real, or press B.

Desktop. WASD move, mouse look (click to capture), Shift run, C crouch, E pick up, click to stab / swing / use the bag (hold), Q or right-click throw, G drop, F swap hands, N free camera.

The show. The Director wants three kills. Give him that and the gate in the east wall opens. CCTV cameras report you to the hunters; a crowbar or a brick shuts them up.
